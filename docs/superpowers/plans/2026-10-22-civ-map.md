# The map: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add a Civ-style hex map to the dashboard that grows from George's honest day score, with claiming, cards, barbarians, golden ages, roads, levels, seasons, a morning turn, a chronicle, a time-lapse, a Claude read and a phone widget.

**Architecture:** A pure replay engine (`js/map/engine.js`) turns the shared document, a city's private map file and George's choice records into the map's state. The UI (`js/ui/map.js`, `js/ui/map-art.js`) draws it as a Daylight tile and a big view. Map files come from the sync repo (`map/index.json`, `map/<city>.json`) and are cached on the device.

**Tech Stack:** Vanilla ES modules, SVG, `node --test`. No new dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-civ-map-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Nothing personal in this repo: no place names, positions, landmark drawings or real data. Tests use an invented town.
- Hammers: 10 per counted done row, 20 when `minutes >= 60`; finished day (done = total, total >= 3) +20.
- Tile cost 40 (30 in a golden age); bank 5; extra claimed by rule (nearest the centre, then lowest q, then lowest r).
- Golden age: from the 3rd finished day in a row; a day with a miss ends it; a day with no counted rows changes nothing.
- Barbarians: one camp per day with a miss, spawned 3 tiles beyond the border towards its target; each turn moves one step; raids on a missed day when adjacent; repair 20 each, paid first; a finished day removes the camp nearest the centre.
- Roads: one step per 10 cardio minutes on the day, remainder carried.
- Levels: after a track's cards are all earned, every 400 more points raises the next placed building of that track one level, up to 3.
- Finds: worth what the map file says (10 to 20), paid once on claim.
- Names only on hover or tap, only for tiles with something on them. Wonder sites are stars until built.
- Every new file is added to `sw.js` SHELL, and `npm run build-release` runs before any push.

## Review Focus

- Two devices claim the same tile, or more tiles than are banked: the replay keeps the first legal ones by time and ignores the rest (test in Task 2).
- A choice dated on a day the replay hasn't reached (a device clock ahead): ignored until that day (Task 2).
- Map file missing, malformed or over the size the device can store: the tile hides and the app still works (Task 4).
- A day before the city's start or after its end, or a day in another city: no effect on this city (Task 2).
- A milestone ticked then unticked on the same day: no wonder card (Task 1, Task 2).

### Task 2: The replay engine

**Files:**
- Create: `js/map/hex.js` (axial helpers), `js/map/engine.js`
- Create: `tests/fixtures/map-town.js` (an invented town: 60 tiles, two found places, one building per track, one wonder, one find)
- Test: `tests/map-engine.test.js`

**Interfaces:**
- `hex.js`: `key(q, r)`, `unkey(k): [q, r]`, `nbrs(q, r): string[]`, `dist(a, b)`, `DIRS`.
- `engine.js`: `replay({ doc, map, today, cityOf = () => map.id, snapshots = false }) → MapState`, `claimable(state): string[]`, `placeable(state, card): string[]`, `cityOfDay(doc, day, maps): string|null`, constants `COST = 40`, `GOLDEN_COST = 30`, `BANK = 5`, `REPAIR = 20`, `LEVEL_STEP = 400`.
- `MapState`: `{ turn, day, owned: Set<k>, visible: Set<k>, ready, pool, cost, golden: {on, run}, track: {learning, sport, culture}, cards: Card[], built: {id: k}, levels: {id: n}, wonders: Set<id>, camps: [{q, r, target}], pillaged: Set<k>, roads: [[k, k]], found: Set<id>, zonesSeen: Set<id>, claimedFinds: Set<k>, chronicle: Entry[], last: Entry|null, history?: Snapshot[] }`; `Card = { kind: 'build'|'wonder', id }`; `Entry = { day, turn, done, total, hammers, golden, events: [{kind, id?, text}], quote }`.
- Replay order per settled day d (start ≤ d < today, d ≤ end, `cityOf(d) === map.id`): apply that day's choices in `at` order (legal only); score the day; repairs; golden; bank/auto-claim; cards and levels; wonder cards from milestones with `doneOn === d`; barbarians; roads; chronicle. Then apply today's choices.

- [ ] Tests (each with the fixture): a finished 6-row day with two long rows gives 100 hammers and 2 tiles banked with 20 over; a 7th banked tile is auto-claimed nearest the centre; a claim record for a non-adjacent tile is ignored; two claims when one is banked keep the earlier `at`; a choice dated after `today` is ignored; three finished days make the third cost 30 and set `golden.on`; a miss ends it; a missed gym row spawns a camp whose `target` is the sport building's zone and which moves one step a turn; a second miss next to the border pillages the owned tile nearest the target and the next 20 hammers repair it; a finished day removes the nearest camp; 25 cardio minutes give 2 road steps and carry 5; Hebrew points past the threshold add the library card, placing it outside its zone is ignored and inside is kept; 400 more learning points after all cards earned raise the library to level 2; a milestone with `doneOn` gives a wonder card placeable only on an owned wonder site; a day in another city or before `start` changes nothing; claiming the find tile adds its bonus once; seeing a found place logs `discovered` once.
- [ ] Run, fail, implement, pass, commit.
